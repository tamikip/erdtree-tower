# Erdtree Tower 2.1.2

A small UI patch for 2.1.1, removing the suspension window that obscured the lower-right corner.

## Suspension overlay removed

- Removed the persistent Save and Suspend [F6] button and its black background.
- Status text in that window is also removed; it no longer shows pending, success or failure messages after pressing F6.
- The F6 shortcut and existing save flow remain. Suspension conditions, multiplayer acknowledgements and revival logic are unchanged.

## Updating

- Includes the spell/incantation affixes, talisman qualities, shared upgrade progress, enemy pools and lobby changes from 2.1.1.
- Launcher version remains 1.6.3; this update changes the game mod.
- Multiplayer suspension remains experimental and has not completed full multiplayer playtesting. Pressing F6 does not prove saving succeeded; do not force-close the game. This patch does not resolve the crashes and disconnects still under investigation.

Thanks for your feedback. If an overlay still obstructs the game, please include a screenshot and your version.
